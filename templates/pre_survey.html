What is the project about?

This research project seeks to explore how various characteristics of technology impact the way users perceive and interact with these systems. Participants will take part in interactive sessions set in a game-like environment, where adjustments will be made to specific features of the systems being used. By observing these interactions, we aim to gain insights into how these technological attributes influence user perceptions and behaviours. The insights gained from this study could play a significant role in guiding the development and improvement of future technologies, enhancing how they are designed and experienced by users across different contexts.
